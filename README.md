# Amazon
Excel project with dashboard , charts and KPIs.

**Amazon E-Commerce Sales & Performance Analysis – Microsoft Excel**

Built an interactive **Amazon E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** in Excel to evaluate sales performance and understand key business trends. Worked with raw sales data by performing **data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing** to prepare it for analysis. Created **calculated fields and business-focused KPIs** to track sales, orders, profit, product performance, customer segments, and regional trends. Used **PivotTables, charts, slicers, and visualizations** to analyze product categories, sales channels, customer purchasing patterns, and overall business performance. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of important e-commerce metrics and helps identify **high-performing products, sales trends, and areas requiring business attention**.
